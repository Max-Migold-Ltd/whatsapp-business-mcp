## WhatsApp Customer Support

When you contact Max-Migold Facility Management on WhatsApp, we collect and use the following information to handle your request:

- **Your WhatsApp details:** your phone number and the profile name shown on your WhatsApp account.
- **Information you give us in the chat:** your location (e.g. La Tour, Lagoon, Seplat Eket), flat number, whether you are making a complaint or giving feedback, the service area concerned, and the description, photos or documents you send.
- **The conversation itself:** the messages exchanged between you, our automated assistant and our support staff, with dates and times.

**Why we use it:** to record and respond to your complaint or feedback, to give you a ticket reference, to let you check the status of your request, and to improve our facility management services.

**How it is handled:** our WhatsApp service is provided through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta). Messages are processed by our own support system and recorded in our internal records on Microsoft SharePoint (Microsoft 365). Your request may be shared with the Max-Migold staff member assigned to handle it. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

**How long we keep it:** we keep support records for as long as needed to resolve your request and for our legitimate record-keeping, after which they are deleted or anonymised. *(Max-Migold to fill in the retention period, e.g. 2 years.)*

**Your rights:** you may ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you from WhatsApp support. To do so, email *(Max-Migold to fill in contact email)*. We will respond in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

## Data deletion instructions (Meta may also ask for this link)

To request deletion of your data collected through Max-Migold's WhatsApp support channel, email *(contact email)* with the subject "Delete my WhatsApp data" and the phone number you used. We will delete your records within 30 days and confirm when done.
